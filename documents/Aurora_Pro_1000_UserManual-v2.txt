2. PRODUCT OVERVIEW & CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEFT EARCUP:
  [ Power Button ]         Press and hold for 3 seconds to turn ON/OFF.
  [ Status LED ]           Displays battery status and pairing state.
  [ USB-C Charging Port ]  Connect the included USB-C cable to charge.
  [ Mic Port ]             3.5mm jack for connecting the detachable boom mic.
  [ Mic Mute Toggle ]      Press to mute/unmute microphone. Red LED indicates mute.

RIGHT EARCUP:
  [ Volume Dial ]          Rotate up/down to adjust audio volume.
  [ EQ Mode Switch ]       Press to cycle through preset equalizer modes.
  [ Mode Switch Button ]   Toggle between Bluetooth and 2.4GHz Wireless modes.

4. GETTING STARTED & CHARGING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before using your Aurora Pro-1000 for the first time, fully charge the battery.

CHARGING INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Plug the provided USB-C cable into the charging port on the left earcup.
2. Connect the other end to a powered USB-A or USB-C port (5V, 1A minimum).
3. The Status LED will display battery status:
   - Solid Amber: Charging in progress
   - Solid Green: Fully charged
   - Blinking Red: Battery low (< 10%)

Note: The headset cannot be used while charging.


5. PAIRING & CONNECTIVITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 BLUETOOTH PAIRING:
1. Ensure the headset is powered off.
2. Press and hold the Power Button for 10 seconds until the Status LED flashes 
   alternating Green and Purple.
3. Open Bluetooth settings on your host device (Smartphone, PC, Mac).
4. Select "Aurora Pro-1000" from the list of available devices.
5. Once connected, the Status LED will turn solid Green.

5.2 2.4GHz WIRELESS USB TRANSCEIVER:
1. Insert the USB-C dongle into an available port on your PC, PlayStation, or Switch.
2. Turn on the headset.
3. Press the Mode Switch Button on the right earcup to switch to 2.4GHz mode.
4. The LED on the USB dongle and headset will turn solid White when connected.


6. AUDIO SETTINGS & EQ MODES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cycle through 4 built-in EQ presets using the EQ Mode Switch on the right earcup:

1. Standard / Flat (Default) - Balanced sound profile for general media and music.
2. Bass Boost - Enhanced low-frequency profile for gaming and movies.
3. Clarity / Vocal - Boosts mid-high frequencies for clear voice communications.
4. Competitive Gaming - Attenuates bass and boosts high frequencies for footsteps 
   and audio cues.


7. CARE, MAINTENANCE & SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Do not expose the device to extreme temperatures (above 45°C / 113°F or below 0°C / 32°F).
- Keep the headset away from liquids, moisture, and high humidity.
- Clean ear cushions using a soft, slightly damp cloth. Isopropyl alcohol may be
  used, but only after the cushions have been detached from the earcups.
- Avoid listening at high volume levels for extended periods to prevent hearing damage.


8. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM: No sound from headset.
SOLUTION:
  - Verify headset is powered on and battery is charged.
  - Ensure correct input mode (Bluetooth vs 2.4GHz) is selected.
  - Check volume dial on right earcup and host device volume settings.

PROBLEM: Microphone is not picking up audio.
SOLUTION:
  - Ensure the boom mic is firmly seated into the 3.5mm mic jack.
  - Check if the Mic Mute button is toggled ON (Red light active).
  - Verify input device settings on host computer or console.

PROBLEM: Frequent wireless disconnections.
SOLUTION:
  - Move closer to the host device / USB dongle.
  - Keep away from 2.4GHz signal interference (Wi-Fi routers, microwaves).
    5GHz Wi-Fi networks do not affect the connection.
