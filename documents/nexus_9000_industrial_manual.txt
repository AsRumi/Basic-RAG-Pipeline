2.2 HIGH-VOLTAGE ELECTRICAL SAFETY GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[DANGER] HIGH VOLTAGE RISK
The Nexus-9000 system utilizes 480V 3-phase alternating current capable of 
delivering lethal electrical shocks. 

1. Only certified, licensed industrial electricians may perform wiring, testing, 
   or internal maintenance inside the high-voltage electrical cabinet.
2. Ensure the main disconnect switch located on the left exterior housing panel 
   is locked in the OFF position before opening the cabinet doors.
3. Allow a minimum of 10 minutes following full power-down for internal variable 
   frequency drive (VFD) capacitors to completely discharge stored energy.
4. Test for zero electrical energy using a verified digital multimeter prior to 
   touching any wire connections or bus bars.

2.3 HIGH-TORQUE MECHANICAL HAZARD PROTOCOLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[WARNING] ROTATING PARTS & PINCH POINTS
The central scaling rotor rotates at speeds up to 3,600 RPM with extreme torque capability.

1. NEVER defeat, bypass, or tamper with the safety interlock switches on the 
   access hatches or intake feed system.
2. Operators MUST wear tight-fitting industrial clothing. Loose sleeves, long hair, 
   necklaces, and gloves are strictly forbidden near the intake conveyor.
3. Keep hands, arms, and tools clear of the internal scaling chamber whenever 
   the system status indicator is illuminated RED or YELLOW.
4. Ensure safety guards over all drive belts and coupling housings remain 
   installed and fully tightened during machine operation.

2.4 EMERGENCY STOP (E-STOP) PROCEDURES & LOCKOUT/TAGOUT (LOTO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Nexus-9000 features three red palm-actuated Emergency Stop buttons:
  - E-STOP 1: Located on the main HMI touchscreen front panel.
  - E-STOP 2: Located near the intake feed hopper assembly.
  - E-STOP 3: Located adjacent to the primary discharge chute.

Pressing ANY E-Stop button instantly cuts power to the main drive motor, applies 
the dual pneumatic emergency brake, and halts all active automation tasks within 
250 milliseconds.

LOCKOUT/TAGOUT (LOTO) PROCEDURE:
Step 1: Notify all nearby operational staff that LOTO is being performed.
Step 2: Initiate a controlled system shutdown from the primary HMI display menu.
Step 3: Rotate the main red handle disconnect valve counter-clockwise to OFF.
Step 4: Place a padlocked lockout clasp over the primary handle bar cutout.
Step 5: Bleed pneumatic pressure by pulling the manual safety relief valve ring.
Step 6: Affix a standardized physical warning tag containing technician name, date, 
        and contact details.

SECTION 3: UNPACKING, INSPECTION & INSTALLATION PROCEDURES
================================================================================

3.1 UNCRATING & INITIAL INSPECTION CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Upon receiving the Nexus-9000 unit:
1. Inspect the exterior wooden crate structure for signs of mishandling or water.
2. Check the Tip-N-Tell and ShockWatch indicator badges on the outer frame. If 
   the indicators show red activation, notify the logistics carrier immediately.
3. Carefully remove top paneling followed by side panels using impact drivers.
4. Cut security strapping and remove protective heavy-duty vapor barrier plastic.
5. Verify that all accessory boxes (Control Pendant, Tool Kit, Cable Hose Array) 
   match the Packing Slip Index.

3.2 SITE PREPARATION & FLOOR LOAD REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Floor Spec: C30/37 structural reinforced concrete slab, minimum 200mm thickness.
- Levelness: Flatness tolerance within ± 2 mm over a 3-meter span.
- Anchor Bolts: Utilize 8x M16 x 150mm heavy-duty expansion mechanical anchors.
- clearance: Provide a minimum 1.2-meter working perimeter around all 4 sides of 
  the unit for ventilation and maintenance doors.

3.3 PNEUMATIC & HYDRAULIC LINE CONNECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Connect a 1/2-inch NPT dry, oil-free compressed air supply line to the main 
   Filter-Regulator-Lubricator (FRL) assembly.
2. Adjust pressure regulator knob to achieve a constant 0.7 MPa operating pressure.
3. Verify condensate auto-drain mechanism functions by pressing test valve pin.

3.4 ELECTRICAL WIRING & GROUNDING INFRASTRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Route 3-phase power through industrial conduit into the top entry gland plate.
2. Connect L1, L2, L3 wires to main terminal blocks L1-IN, L2-IN, and L3-IN.
3. Connect Earth Ground line to the central copper grounding bolt with a cable 
   having cross-sectional area >= 16 mm². Ground impedance must read < 1.0 Ohm.

SECTION 4: COMPONENT OVERVIEW & SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

4.1 MAIN PROCESSING CHAMBER & ROTOR ASSEMBLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The core of the Nexus-9000 consists of a precision-milled cylindrical chamber 
containing a balanced 8-vane titanium-nitride coated scaling rotor. Material 
entering the top port is metered, measured, and accelerated through the chamber 
wall where multi-point piezoelectric pressure sensors measure mass flow with high accuracy.

4.2 FEED HOPPER & AUTOMATED CONVEYOR INTAKE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The feed system utilizes a variable-frequency vibratory feed pan combined with 
an overhead metering gate. Dual optical laser sensors mounted across the hopper 
mouth monitor material height to prevent bridging and over-fill situations.

4.3 PNEUMATIC ACTUATORS & SOLENOID CONTROL VALVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
High-cycle pneumatic cylinders actuate the upper intake gate, lower discharge valve, 
and automated sampling ports. All pneumatic functions are driven by an integrated 
24V DC solenoid valve manifold operating via direct CANopen fieldbus communications.

4.4 INTEGRATED SENSOR NETWORK & FEEDBACK LOOPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Load Cells: 4x Strain-gauge load cells (IP68) arranged in a full bridge circuit.
- Temperature Sensors: 2x PT100 RTD probes measuring rotor bearing temperature.
- Vibration Monitoring: Tri-axial MEMS accelerometer evaluating rotor imbalance.
- Speed Encoder: 1024-PPR optical rotary encoder attached to motor shaft.

5.2 INITIAL CALIBRATION SEQUENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Clean all residual material out of the main scaling chamber and discharge chute.
2. From the HMI Main Menu, tap [ SETTINGS ] -> [ CALIBRATION ] -> [ ZERO TARE ].
3. Allow the system to collect 500 sensor samples over a 10-second window.
4. Place a certified 50.00 kg calibration test weight onto the internal load platform.
5. Key in "50.00" on the touchscreen keypad and tap [ EXECUTE SPAN CALIBRATION ].
6. Verify calibration error margin reads < 0.02%. Tap [ SAVE CALIBRATION PROFILE ].

5.3 PARAMETER CONFIGURATION & CUSTOM PRESETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Operators can store up to 64 distinct material profiles.

Configurable Preset Parameters:
  - Preset Name: (String, max 24 chars)
  - Target Rotor RPM: (Range: 50 - 3600)
  - Intake Feed Rate: (10% to 100%)
  - Target Batch Mass: (Range: 1.0 kg - 999.0 kg)
  - Settling Time Window: (0.5 sec - 10.0 sec)
  - High-Accuracy Fine Dosing Threshold: (80% - 98%)

5.4 AUTOMATED BATCH PROCESSING WORKFLOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When running in AUTO mode:
1. System executes pre-start safety loop check (Guard doors, E-Stops, Air Pressure).
2. Discharge gate closes and verifies full seal.
3. Rotor accelerates to programmed Target RPM.
4. Intake vibratory feeder activates at coarse feed rate.
5. Upon reaching fine dosing threshold, vibratory feeder reduces speed.
6. When target mass is reached within tolerance, intake gate snaps shut.
7. System waits for vibration settling time window to expire.
8. Mass data is logged to internal memory and network database.
9. Discharge gate opens, emptying material to downstream process.

SECTION 6: PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE & ROUTINE SERVICING
================================================================================

6.1 DAILY PRE-OPERATIONAL INSPECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Inspect power cord and control cable conduits for cuts, abrasions, or damage.
[ ] Check compressed air pressure gauge on FRL unit (must read 0.6 - 0.8 MPa).
[ ] Inspect feed hopper and scaling chamber for foreign debris or buildup.
[ ] Test operation of all E-Stop buttons and safety door switch interlocks.

6.2 WEEKLY LUBRICATION & MECHANICAL TENSION ADJUSTMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Apply Synthetic Lithium Grease (NLGI Grade 2) to main shaft bearing grease zerts.
[ ] Check drive belt tension using ultrasonic tension meter (target: 65 Hz ± 5 Hz).
[ ] Drain accumulated moisture bowl on pneumatic filter assembly.
[ ] Wipe clean optical laser sensor lenses using isopropyl alcohol wipes.

6.3 MONTHLY SENSOR CALIBRATION & FIRMWARE DIAGNOSTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Perform full multi-point scale calibration using certified weights.
[ ] Inspect pneumatic hose lines for micro-fissures or air leakage sounds.
[ ] Measure motor winding insulation resistance with 500V Megohmmeter (> 5 Megohms).
[ ] Backup system configuration presets and error logs to USB storage drive.

6.4 QUARTERLY DEEP CLEANING & WEAR-AND-TEAR AUDITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Remove rotor chamber end-plate; inspect titanium-nitride vanes for wear or pitting.
[ ] Replace rotor chamber silicone lip seals (Part # NX-7022-S).
[ ] Inspect rubber anti-vibration isolation mounts for cracking or degradation.
[ ] Update NexusOS firmware to latest vendor release version.

SECTION 7: TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE & ERROR CODES
================================================================================

7.1 SYSTEM DIAGNOSTICS LOGIC CHART
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Condition: Machine will not turn on.
  -> Check main breaker on electrical wall unit.
  -> Verify 24V DC internal power supply indicator light is ON.
  -> Inspect control transformer primary fuse F1 (5A, Slow-Blow).

Condition: Rotor fails to achieve target speed.
  -> Inspect VFD display panel inside main electrical box for error codes.
  -> Verify drive belt tension is set correctly.
  -> Check chamber for mechanical obstruction or material jam.

7.2 CRITICAL HARDWARE ERROR CODES (E-100 TO E-499)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E-101 [EMERGENCY STOP ACTIVATED]
  Cause: One or more E-Stop switches have been depressed.
  Action: Release E-Stop button; tap [ RESET ALARM ] on HMI.

E-104 [DOOR INTERLOCK TRIPPED]
  Cause: Rotor access hatch or hopper service door opened while system active.
  Action: Close and latch all safety doors; verify door switch LED is GREEN.

E-210 [LOAD CELL OVERRANGE DETECTED]
  Cause: Weight applied to scaling chamber exceeds maximum 500 kg limit.
  Action: Immediately clear excessive weight from intake hopper.

E-302 [MOTOR OVER-TEMPERATURE TRIP]
  Cause: Main motor temperature exceeded 105°C for longer than 10 seconds.
  Action: Allow motor to cool down; inspect cooling fan shroud for blockages.

E-412 [PNEUMATIC PRESSURE LOW]
  Cause: Supply pressure dropped below 0.5 MPa threshold.
  Action: Check external air compressor line and primary intake valve.

7.3 SOFT WARNING CODES & OPERATIONAL THRESHOLD ALERTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
W-501 [TARE VARIANCE WARNING]
  Cause: Zero offset drifted by > 0.5% since last calibration.
  Action: Perform chamber cleaning and execute ZERO TARE sequence.

W-604 [ROTOR IMBALANCE DETECTED]
  Cause: Vibration accelerometer reading exceeds 4.5 mm/s RMS threshold.
  Action: Inspect rotor vanes for uneven material buildup or missing hardware.

7.4 MECHANICAL JAM RECOVERY PROCEDURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Engage Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) protocol as specified in Section 2.4.
2. Open rotor chamber front access cover using the specialized hex key tool.
3. Using protective cut-resistant gloves, carefully extract jammed foreign object.
4. Clean interior wall surfaces with non-scratch scraper.
5. Close access door, ensure gasket is seated properly, and tighten latch bolts.
6. Remove LOTO locks, restore power, and run system at 100 RPM test speed.
